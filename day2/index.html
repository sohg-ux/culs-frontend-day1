Good to know

Small choices make a better day

  • Wear comfortable shoes. Prague’s historic centre has many cobblestones.
  • Carry a refillable bottle. Take regular breaks, especially in warm weather.
  • Respect the neighbourhood. Keep noise low in residential streets.
